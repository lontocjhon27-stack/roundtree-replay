What You Learned In This Session

How to stop chasing money, make mathematical decisions, and build a business that banks fight to lend to.

01

The $5.1 Million Math Problem

The American dream now costs about $5.1M — roughly $126K a year for 40 years. Even a PhD averages about $81K. Earning more won't close that gap. Leverage will, and so will mathematical decisions in place of emotional ones.

02

See Capital Differently

The 1% deploy capital into assets: income-producing property, equipment, marketing with a real ROI. Consumers beg for capital and spend it on liabilities. Watch the ROI, not the interest rate.

03

Banks Read Data, Not Character

Five things make you fundable: proper structure, optimized personal credit, business credit reporting, a clean bank profile, and a FICO SBSS score built with purpose. Good intentions don't count.

04

FICO SBSS: 160 Or Higher

It's 35% owner credit (aim for 720+), 30% business financials, 20% the business itself (industry, location, website, so skip "consulting"), and 15% the business bureaus. Below 160, the real money stays out of reach.

05

Depreciate, Don't Just Expense

Make $100K and expense all of it, and the bank sees $0. Depreciate it, and the bank still sees $100K. Then use structured debt like 0% business cards to replace costly MCA loans and free up cash flow.

06

Build The Legacy Loop

Buy, borrow, repeat. Borrow against assets instead of selling them, and let the assets cover the payment. Tactics like a mortgage recast can lower a payment without refinancing into today's rates.
